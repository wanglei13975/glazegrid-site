GLAZEGRID · PRIVACY

Your firing notes stay on your device.

GlazeGrid: Kiln Notes stores firing batches, tile details, observations and next-test notes locally on the device. The V1 app does not require an account and does not send those notes to a developer-controlled server.

Data collection

GlazeGrid does not collect personal data, contact data, location, photos, camera or microphone recordings. It does not use advertising, tracking, analytics SDKs or remote sync.

Purchases

The optional GlazeGrid Pro lifetime unlock is processed by Apple through StoreKit. GlazeGrid uses verified StoreKit transaction state to control access and does not receive or store payment credentials.

Sharing

If you choose Share Summary, iOS presents the system share sheet with a summary generated from your local records. GlazeGrid does not choose the destination.
